# 2D Visualization Requirements

**Status:** Draft for review
**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Applies to:** Initial VGA 2D vertical slice
**License:** MIT

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the minimum shared guarantees needed to discover the
initial two-dimensional visual workflow through implementation. It is not a
complete design for every future 2D entity or interaction. Findings from the
vertical slice may revise these draft requirements before acceptance.

Algebra-specific interpretation produces renderer-independent semantic entities
before this layer. The visualizer consumes those entities and never backend
algebra values.

## 2. Foundation rendering

- **VIZ2D-001:** The initial visualizer shall provide a two-dimensional
  mathematical viewport with visible origin and axes.
- **VIZ2D-002:** It shall render supported points, positioned vectors,
  positioned oriented areas, labels, appearance, visibility, and lists through
  one primitive pipeline.
- **VIZ2D-003:** Algebraic results shall not depend on viewport state.
- **VIZ2D-004:** A positioned vector with a nonzero grade-one value shall render
  as an oriented segment from its position to its derived head. Direction shall
  not be conveyed by color alone. The all-zero multivector shall receive the
  same canonical scalar interpretation regardless of source construction and
  shall retain a textual state without a spatial vector marker.
- **VIZ2D-005:** Render primitives shall contain no algebra backend value or
  algebra identifier.
- **VIZ2D-006:** Unsupported entities and render truncation shall be reported in
  text without altering the evaluated mathematical result.
- **VIZ2D-007:** Construction provenance and object appearance may select
  presentation defaults, but shall not change the standard geometric
  interpretation of an owned multivector value.
- **VIZ2D-008:** A nonzero pure VGA(2) bivector shall render at its separately
  evaluated position as an oriented loop whose mathematical enclosed area is
  the absolute bivector value. Positive and negative values shall communicate
  counter-clockwise and clockwise orientation without relying on color. When
  safely available, direct `V ^ W` construction provenance may instead select
  the corresponding oriented parallelogram, preserving the same area
  convention. Text and accessible SVG descriptions shall identify the signed
  value, orientation, area, and position.
- **VIZ2D-009:** A list whose evaluated elements are all finite or ideal
  points, at least one of them finite, shall offer an item-level connection:
  none (the default), an open polyline, or a closed loop. A connection joins
  consecutive elements in evaluated list order, whatever produced the list:
  `n - 1` links for `n` elements, plus the last-to-first link for a closed
  loop of at least three elements. Two finite points join with a straight
  segment; a finite point and an ideal point join with the ray from the
  finite point in the ideal point's direction, clipped to the viewport; two
  consecutive ideal points join along the line at infinity and draw nothing.
  Empty and singleton lists draw no link, repeated finite points draw
  zero-length segments, and only the rendered prefix of a truncated list is
  joined. Links share the item's visibility and color.
  Point markers and their labels are kept by default. A connected list may
  hide its markers to show the links alone: finite point markers and ideal
  point glyphs are hidden, finite point labels remain at the points, and a
  list without a connection always draws its markers. The
  connection is presentation only: it never changes point values, and lists
  holding other entities, or only ideal points, neither offer nor apply it.

## 3. Viewport

- **VIEW2D-001:** Screen and mathematical coordinates shall be connected by one
  explicit, testable viewport transform.
- **VIEW2D-002:** Pan, zoom, and layout resize shall not change mathematical
  values or coordinates.
- **VIEW2D-003:** Recentering the view shall center the mathematical origin at
  the current scale; resetting the view shall center the origin at the
  documented default scale. The viewport's recenter control and the `0` key
  recenter and the `Home` key resets; typing a zoom level of 100 % restores
  the default scale around the current center.
- **VIEW2D-004:** Continuous zoom shall remain centered on the pointer's
  mathematical coordinate and shall be bounded from 0.001 through 1 000 000
  reference pixels per mathematical unit. Reset uses 72 reference pixels per
  unit, shown as 100 %. A zoom level typed as a percentage shall be clamped to
  the same bounds and centered on the viewport center; text that is not a
  positive percentage returns to 100 %.
- **VIEW2D-005:** Major grid spacing shall follow a deterministic 1–2–5 × 10^n
  progression with bounded subdivisions and generated line counts. Numeric
  labels shall use stable precision without floating-point artifacts.
- **VIEW2D-006:** Grid, axes and axis labels, graduations, and object scale shall
  be independently controllable through persistent view state. View commands
  shall not create or replace mathematical undo-history entries.
- **VIEW2D-007:** Pointer and keyboard input shall provide equivalent pan, zoom,
  and reset outcomes. Resizing shall retain the visible mathematical center.
- **VIEW2D-008:** A button at the top right of the expression panel shall
  collapse the panel fully to the left so that the canvas takes the freed
  width; a button at the left edge of the canvas shall expand it again at its
  previous width. Collapsing moves focus to the expand button and expanding
  returns it to the collapse button; both buttons have accessible names and
  `aria-expanded`. Evaluation, animation, and viewport interaction continue
  while the panel is collapsed. At phone width, where the panel is stacked
  above the canvas, collapsing hides it the same way. The collapsed state is a
  local view preference, restored on the next visit like the viewport lock and
  tolerant of unavailable storage; it is not part of the document, its
  history, its export, or scene links. There is no keyboard shortcut. The
  slide respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Without a visualizer the panel
  cannot be collapsed: a document without a canvas (imported, dropped,
  restored, or linked) reopens a collapsed panel and stores the preference as
  expanded, and focus left in the removed canvas moves to the panel's
  **Show canvas** button (VIEW2D-009).
- **VIEW2D-009:** A button at the top left of the canvas shall collapse the
  canvas to the right into the **NoViz** calculator: the document's
  `visualizerId` becomes `null`, its viewport `none`, and `positionEnabled`
  `false`, the only state in which positions are off. Nothing is rendered and
  no render primitives are built; the expression panel takes the whole width
  and shows the mode as text. Position fields, visibility buttons, label
  fields, the appearance menu, and the rendering-only settings (grid, axes,
  graduations, object size) are hidden; appearance stays in the document,
  dormant, and the swatch keeps showing the object's color. Evaluation,
  diagnostics, scalar controls and playback, normalization, history,
  persistence, import and export, and the theme are unchanged. A button at the
  right edge of the panel brings the canvas back with the visualizer and
  viewport the session left, or the algebra's standard visualizer at the
  default view, and sets `positionEnabled` to `true`. NoViz is document state:
  it is saved, exported, and carried by scene links; switching does not create
  an undo entry (VIEW2D-006). Focus moves to the button that reverses the
  switch. Entering NoViz expands a collapsed panel, and changing the algebra
  keeps a NoViz document in NoViz. Undo and redo never change the mode, and
  a snapshot of another algebra keeps that algebra's visualizer, so the
  canvas always matches the document's algebra; the view kept for the return
  is used only for the algebra it was left in, and only for the open
  document: replacing the document (import, drop, or any other opening)
  forgets it. Any document without a canvas is shown as the NoViz
  calculator, so that the button bringing the canvas back is always offered:
  a document with only one of the two NoViz markers, an unknown visualizer,
  or an unavailable interpretation. That button then sets the algebra's
  standard visualizer, keeps a stored two-dimensional viewport, and selects
  the algebra's standard interpretation, in an undoable step, when the stored
  one is unavailable.
- **VIEW2D-010:** The band at the bottom of the expression panel,
  **? Expression reference** with a chevron and the algebra badge, shall open
  and close the expression reference under it, inside the panel and never as
  a modal; the band reports `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and the
  canvas stays visible and usable. The clear-all control sits at the bottom
  of the expression list, right-aligned. While the reference is open, a
  handle above the band shares the panel height between the list and the
  reference by pointer drag and Up and Down arrows (default 58 %, from 25 %
  to 85 %); the height is a local view preference kept like the viewport
  lock, tolerant of unavailable storage, and never read or written by a
  temporary scene-link session. List and reference scroll independently.
  The reference has two categories. **General expressions** holds what is
  true in every algebra (values and names, lists and ranges, operators,
  inverse, functions, precedence) and ends with **Style**, which lists only
  the values of the appearance settings. **PGA expressions** (or **VGA
  expressions**) holds the active algebra's own syntax, supplied by the
  algebra definition: its **Primitives** (each with how it is written and
  one short line, then normalization), values, properties, functions and
  positioning. The reference has no search, section chips or per-entry
  algebra tags. Section titles are sticky, and examples show their result
  with a named **+** button that inserts the example, with its position, as
  a new focused expression. Escape inside the reference closes it and
  returns focus to the band. Entries hold a syntax, at most one short line,
  and at most two examples; the reference shows no default colors. At phone width the reference keeps its place
  under the list, at full width, without horizontal scrolling.

## 4. Interaction

The interaction requirements below have **Milestone** commitment and apply only
to the VGA 2D Visual Workflow milestone.

- **INTERACT2D-001:** Double-clicking empty viewport space shall create a named
  declaration of the active interpretation's creation constructor (a VGA(2)
  vector, a PGA(2) point) using the mathematical coordinate under the pointer.
  Its name is the first one not declared in the document among `A` to `Z`, then
  `A1` to `Z1`, `A2` to `Z2`, and so on. Rendered entities are not directly
  selectable by clicking them.
- **INTERACT2D-002:** Viewport creation shall use the shared inverse viewport
  transform and deterministic source precision. Pan, zoom, reset, and layout
  changes shall not alter the mathematical coordinate represented by a click.
- **INTERACT2D-003:** Generated declaration names shall not collide with an
  existing declaration, built-in, or reserved VGA name.
- **INTERACT2D-004:** One creation gesture shall produce one semantic insertion
  command and at most one document-history entry. Focus moves to the created
  expression; undo returns focus to the viewport and redo restores expression
  focus.
- **INTERACT2D-005:** Double-clicking a rendered object shall not create an
  expression. Creation success and refusal shall be announced in text to
  assistive technology.
- **INTERACT2D-006:** Eligible vector and bivector bases shall expose a shared
  solid point with a translucent movable contour. Moving a base shall edit only
  its position source; a locked base shall retain only the solid point.
- **INTERACT2D-007:** An eligible direct vector head shall retain its arrowhead
  and expose a thin unfilled interaction ring only on hover or keyboard focus.
  Moving the head shall edit its vector components with the base fixed.
- **INTERACT2D-008:** Pointer manipulation shall use capture and one preview
  transaction. A drag starts only once the pointer has moved 3 CSS pixels from
  the press; a press released before that is a click and writes nothing.
  Completion creates one undo entry; Escape, pointer cancellation,
  or lost capture restores the exact pre-gesture document. Undoing or redoing a
  manipulation entry keeps focus in the viewport: on the handle a keyboard
  gesture moved when it still exists, otherwise on the canvas.
- **INTERACT2D-009:** Every movable base or head shall provide keyboard arrow
  movement with small and Shift-modified large increments and textual status
  announcements. The small increment is the 1–2–5 step covering at least
  7 screen pixels at the current zoom, the large one ten times that, and the
  announced position matches the rewritten source.
- **INTERACT2D-010:** Rendered object bodies and list-rendered copies shall not
  become direct manipulation or selection targets. Coincident and overlapping
  interaction targets shall resolve deterministically: the handles of the
  selected object are drawn above the others and take the press, and the
  others keep document order. The selected object is the one whose expression
  row has focus or the pointer, otherwise the last object a pointer
  manipulated in the viewport; the order does not change during a drag or
  while a handle has keyboard focus. Keyboard manipulation does not change it,
  since Tab reaches every handle. The tab order of the handles follows the
  drawing order, so the handles of the selected object come last.
- **INTERACT2D-011:** Dragging an eligible object base within a fixed
  screen-space radius of a named vector or bivector base, or a named vector
  head, shall preview and create a dynamic `.position` or `.head`
  position-source reference. Viewport zoom shall not change the acquisition
  radius.
- **INTERACT2D-012:** Anchor acquisition shall exclude the dragged object,
  anonymous and list-rendered objects, and any relationship that would produce
  an invalid or cyclic position dependency. Refusal shall preserve the exact
  pre-gesture document and be announced textually.
- **INTERACT2D-013:** Anchor preview shall use shape as well as color, retain the
  current candidate through a larger release radius, and resolve ties by stable
  target identity and anchor kind.
  A base drag shall not take an anchor that lies within the release radius of
  its press until the pointer has left that radius once, so that a short drag
  separates coinciding or linked objects instead of linking them back.
- **INTERACT2D-014:** Dragging a linked base away shall replace the relationship
  with its resolved literal position. Linking and unlinking shall each form one
  atomic undo/redo entry.
- **INTERACT2D-015:** A focused movable base shall support cycling valid anchors
  with Enter and unlinking with Delete or Backspace, with textual announcements
  equivalent to the pointer workflow.

## 5. Deferred design

The initial slice does not commit to direct object selection, rotors, arbitrary
higher-grade entities, cameras, per-element list appearance, or
user-authored interpretations. These receive requirements only when prototypes
or a delivery milestone need them.

A one-dimensional visualizer is also deferred and is not a prerequisite for
this document or the initial public workflow.
